About Yourself

About Your Profession

WHAT DO YOU LOVE MOST ABOUT THE CLOUD AND DEVOPS FIELD?

Everyone is moving to Cloud. This is no longer an option for organizations; you have to migrate to the Cloud or be left behind in the race. It is equally and critically important to have a highly available and stable network for your Cloud infrastructure.

HOW DO YOU MOTIVATE YOURSELF TO DO THE BEST FOR YOURSELF AND THE ORGANIZATION?

I always try to be aware of new tools launched in the market. It is exciting to master them and focus on delivering the benefits of those tools to our customers, despite having to overcome the minimal support you get from the open-source community.

WHAT KINDS OF RESOURCES HAVE YOU TAKEN ADVANTAGE OF WHILE ON OUR TEAM?

I was encouraged to do my certifications by the leaders here. I have completed AWS certifications. Apart from learning for myself, I have also shared my knowledge through training sessions for the team here.

WHAT IS ON YOUR WISH LIST FOR THE NEXT 12 MONTHS AT CLOUDIFYOPS?

To manage a team of 30 members.

Upskilling and Certifications

WHAT IS THE ONE THING YOU LEARNED IN THE LAST MONTH RELATED TO YOUR WORK?

Jumpcloud, Grafana with Mimir (cost-effective option for monitoring and log management).

WHAT DO YOU LIKE ABOUT THE LEARNING/TRAINING INITIATIVES AT CLOUDIFYOPS?

We have changed the way junior training program initiatives are handled by our Director of Technology, Kumaradas. We now have tailored material on various technologies that new joiners can follow, along with pre-recorded videos and GitLab repositories for practice exercises.

WHAT CERTIFICATION ARE YOU PREPARING FOR NOW? OR WHAT WAS THE MOST RECENT CERTIFICATION YOU OBTAINED?

I recently obtained my AWS Certified Solution Architect Professional certification.

Journey with CloudifyOps

HOW HAVE YOU GROWN PROFESSIONALLY WHILE ON OUR TEAM?

I joined CloudifyOps as an Associate Manager - IT and am now a Technical Architect. I am leading a team of 10 across IT Support, Cloud, and DevOps. Over the years, I have handled and learned from various challenges across different projects.

WHAT HAVE BEEN YOUR BIGGEST ACCOMPLISHMENTS ON OUR TEAM? WHERE HAVE YOU MADE THE BIGGEST IMPACT?

I enabled a customer to become HIPAA compliant. This was critical to the organization as they work with health-related information and should conform to relevant regulations.

HOW HAS CLOUDIFYOPS HELPED YOU DEVELOP YOURSELF PERSONALLY (SOFT SKILLS)?

From an IT Administrator to a manager, my growth has been tremendous. To lead a team, you need to be empathetic and motivating. I developed this focus on individuals apart from guiding them on the technology front.

WHAT WAS THE MOST FUN MOMENT WITH THE TEAM?

It was at the 2021 annual celebrations in Pallet, Bangalore. During office hours and in the office, we don't go beyond work-related discussions very often. This celebration is one of my best memories of having a lot of fun with the team.
